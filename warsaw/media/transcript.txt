## An agent

[1:00] That loop is an agent. Not a chatbot. A teammate that can try something, check it, and try again.

## Graphs

## Two big ideas

[1:36] So, two big ideas. Agentic engineering is designing the loop: what an agent knows, what it may touch, and how it checks itself.
[1:45] Graph engineering is designing the connections: who hands work to whom, what waits for what, and where the final yes or no gets made.

## 01 Define done

[2:36] One. Define done before you start.

## 02 Context

[2:57] Two. Context is the map.

## 03 Split
